BIBLICAL WEALTH CREATION Day 1

The Divine Gift of Wealth Capacity

"But remember the LORD your God, for it is he who gives you the ability to produce wealth, and so confirms his covenant, which he swore to your ancestors, as it is today." — Deuteronomy 8:18

1. Biblical & Historical Context

Moses spoke these words to Israel on the verge of Canaan. Israel was transitioning from manna in the wilderness to agriculture, real estate, and trade. God warned that prosperity often breeds spiritual amnesia.

2. Spiritual Interpretation

Wealth is not created by human arrogance or luck alone; it originates in the cognitive, physical, and circumstantial gifts granted by God. Wealth capability is tied to God's covenantal commitment.

3. Connection to Money & Wealth

True financial power is not passive; it is productive. Money is a tool resulting from disciplined stewardship, skill development, and strategic value exchange in the market.

4. Business & Entrepreneurship

Enterprise is a divine calling to solve problems for others. Entrepreneurship multiplies resources and provides employment, reflecting God's creative order.
